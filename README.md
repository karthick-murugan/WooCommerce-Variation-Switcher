# WooCommerce Variation Switcher

WooCommerce Variation Switcher allows customers to **change product variations directly on the Cart and Checkout pages**.

Normally in WooCommerce, if a customer wants to change a variation (like **size, color, or style**), they must remove the product from the cart and go back to the product page. This plugin removes that extra step by letting customers switch variations instantly without leaving the cart or checkout.

## Features

* Change product variations directly in the **Cart page**
* Update variations on the **Checkout page**
* Automatically updates **price, availability, and totals**
* Works with **WooCommerce variable products**
* Improves the overall **shopping experience**

## Example

If a customer adds a **T-shirt (Size: Medium)** to the cart but later wants **Size: Large**, they can simply change the variation in the cart or checkout without removing the products.
